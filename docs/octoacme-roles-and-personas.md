# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Receive feature specs and acceptance criteria; collaborate on trade-offs
- **Project Managers**: Report progress and blockers; participate in planning
- **UX/UI Designers**: Implement design specifications; provide technical feasibility feedback
- **QA Lead**: Coordinate testing strategy and acceptance criteria validation
- **Technical Writer**: Clarify technical details for documentation

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Project Managers**: Partner on timeline and release planning; align on risk mitigation
- **Developers**: Clarify requirements; validate technical feasibility
- **UX/UI Designers**: Define user experience requirements and success metrics
- **Business Analyst**: Validate requirements and business rules alignment
- **QA Lead**: Define acceptance criteria and quality expectations

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Align on roadmap and release schedules; manage dependencies
- **Developers**: Track progress; identify and escalate blockers
- **Scrum Master**: Coordinate Agile ceremonies; remove team impediments
- **Business Analyst**: Validate scope and timeline feasibility
- **Stakeholders**: Provide regular updates and escalate critical risks

---

## UX/UI Designer

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers create intuitive, accessible, and visually appealing user experiences. They collaborate with Product Managers and Developers to ensure designs meet user needs and technical constraints.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Ensure accessibility and design consistency across features
- Collaborate with Developers on implementation feasibility
- Iterate designs based on user feedback and metrics

### Goals
- Deliver user-centered designs that drive adoption and satisfaction
- Reduce user friction and support burden
- Establish and maintain design system consistency

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews with stakeholders and developers
- User research findings and recommendations
- Design specifications and component documentation
- Feedback and iteration cycles with QA and Product Managers

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Define user experience requirements and success metrics
- **Developers**: Validate technical feasibility; provide design specifications
- **Business Analyst**: Understand user workflows and business requirements
- **QA Lead**: Coordinate user acceptance testing and usability validation
- **Technical Writer**: Ensure documentation matches user interface changes

---

## Scrum Master

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate Agile ceremonies, remove team impediments, and foster a culture of continuous improvement. They enable the team to execute effectively within the sprint framework.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help resolve team blockers and dependencies
- Track sprint metrics and team velocity
- Coach team members on Agile practices
- Escalate organizational impediments that block the team

### Goals
- Maximize team velocity and delivery predictability
- Foster psychological safety and continuous improvement
- Remove organizational barriers to effective execution

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups with delivery team
- Sprint planning and retrospective facilitation
- Blocker escalation and removal coordination
- Metrics and velocity reporting to Project Manager

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Project Managers**: Report on sprint health and velocity; escalate blocking issues
- **Developers**: Facilitate Agile ceremonies; help unblock technical challenges
- **Product Managers**: Coordinate backlog refinement and sprint planning
- **Business Analyst**: Clarify requirements during sprint execution
- **QA Lead**: Ensure quality criteria are met within sprint commitments

---

## Technical Writer

### Role Summary
Technical Writers create and maintain clear, accurate documentation that enables customers and team members to understand and use features. They collaborate with Developers and Product Managers to ensure documentation quality and completeness.

### Responsibilities
- Create user guides, API documentation, and release notes
- Maintain documentation consistency and accuracy
- Collaborate with engineers to capture technical details
- Review documentation for clarity and completeness
- Update docs as features evolve and feedback is received

### Goals
- Reduce support burden through clear, self-service documentation
- Enable faster adoption and reduce onboarding time
- Maintain a single source of truth for product information

### Typical Communication
- Documentation reviews with Developers and Product Managers
- Release note coordination before launches
- Feedback from support and customer success teams
- Documentation updates tied to feature releases

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Clarify technical implementation details; validate accuracy
- **Product Managers**: Understand feature benefits and user scenarios
- **UX/UI Designers**: Align documentation with user interface changes
- **QA Lead**: Document test scenarios and known issues
- **Project Manager**: Coordinate documentation timelines with releases

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge stakeholders and the delivery team by gathering, clarifying, and validating requirements. They ensure features solve actual business problems and align with organizational goals.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements from stakeholders
- Map business processes and identify improvement opportunities
- Validate that proposed solutions align with business goals
- Create detailed requirement specifications and acceptance criteria
- Identify and mitigate risks related to scope and feasibility

### Goals
- Ensure alignment between business needs and delivery outcomes
- Reduce rework and scope creep through clear requirements
- Improve stakeholder satisfaction and buy-in

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder interviews and requirement workshops
- Requirements documentation and specifications
- Traceability matrices and scope change requests
- Acceptance criteria validation with QA and developers

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Refine business requirements into product requirements
- **Project Managers**: Validate scope and timeline feasibility
- **Developers**: Clarify technical requirements and edge cases
- **QA Lead**: Define test scenarios based on business rules
- **Stakeholders**: Gather requirements and validate delivered solutions

---

## QA Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads define quality strategies, coordinate testing efforts, and ensure features meet acceptance criteria before release. They balance thoroughness with delivery velocity.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and approach for features
- Create and maintain test plans and test cases
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts
- Report quality metrics and test coverage
- Validate acceptance criteria and release readiness

### Goals
- Ensure features meet quality standards before release
- Reduce defects in production
- Provide confidence in release readiness through comprehensive testing

### Typical Communication
- Test strategy and plan documentation
- Daily quality metrics and test progress updates
- Bug reports with reproduction steps and severity
- Release readiness assessments and sign-off
- Test automation recommendations to developers

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Align on acceptance criteria and quality expectations
- **Developers**: Coordinate test automation strategy; validate bug fixes
- **UX/UI Designers**: Conduct usability testing and validate design implementation
- **Business Analyst**: Validate test scenarios against business requirements
- **Project Manager**: Report on quality status and release readiness
